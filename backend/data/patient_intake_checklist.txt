Standard Patient Intake Checklist

Administrative details:
- patient name
- date of birth
- contact number
- visit date
- provider or clinic location

Clinical intake details:
- chief complaint or reason for visit
- history of present illness, including onset, duration, severity, and triggers
- relevant past medical history
- current medications, dose, and frequency when available
- allergies and reaction details
- vital signs such as blood pressure, heart rate, temperature, oxygen saturation, weight, and height
- relevant review of systems

Documentation guidance:
- Mark missing fields clearly instead of inventing details.
- Use patient-reported language for subjective symptoms.
- Separate objective measurements from subjective complaints.
- Avoid diagnosis or treatment recommendations unless already documented in the notes.
